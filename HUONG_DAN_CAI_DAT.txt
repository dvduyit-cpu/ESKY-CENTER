PHAN MEM QUAN LY CHI TIEU KPI
============================

1. Chay CAI_DAT.bat de cai thu vien Laravel.
2. Mo phpMyAdmin -> Import -> chon DATABASE_KPI_MANAGER.sql.
   Hoac chay IMPORT_DATABASE.bat.
3. Chay CHAY_PHAN_MEM.bat.
4. Mo http://127.0.0.1:8000
